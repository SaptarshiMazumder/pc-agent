Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

This policy covers comfypenguin.com, operated by Nakama AI ("we", "us"). It says what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how to make us delete it.

We do not sell your data, and we do not run advertising. There are no third-party trackers, no ad pixels and no analytics profile built about you.

1. What we collect

When you sign in

Sign-in is through Google. Google tells us your email address, name and profile picture; that is all we ask for and all we receive. We never see your Google password.

When you use the agent

  • What you type — your messages to the agent, and its replies.
  • Files you upload — images and workflow files you give it to work with.
  • Your ComfyUI connection details — the URL of your instance and any credential you configure so the agent can reach it. This is stored so you do not have to re-enter it. It is used for one purpose: talking to your instance.
  • Operational logs — timestamps, error messages, which model was called and how many tokens it used. This is what the credit balance is computed from and what we read when something breaks.

When you pay

Payments are processed by our payment gateway. Your card number, CVV, UPI PIN and bank credentials are entered on the gateway's page and never reach our servers. We receive and keep only the payment reference, the amount, the currency, the status and the time — enough to credit your account and to issue a refund.

2. Why we collect it

  • To run the service you asked for — building and running your workflows.
  • To keep your account and your credit balance correct.
  • To take payments and issue refunds.
  • To find and fix faults, and to prevent abuse and fraud.
  • To answer you when you write to support.

We do not use your conversations or your uploads to train AI models, and we do not give them to anyone to train on.

3. Who else sees it

Only the processors that make the service work, and only what each one needs:

AI model providers
Your messages and uploads are sent to the AI model providers that run the models the agent uses — which may include OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and Moonshot. This is unavoidable: it is how the agent thinks.
Your own ComfyUI instance
Prompts and images are sent to the ComfyUI server you nominate. If that is a rented GPU, that host's privacy terms apply to what happens there, not ours.
Payment gateway
Razorpay, for taking payments and issuing refunds. Their own privacy policy governs the card details you enter on their page.
Hosting
Amazon Web Services. Your account data and conversations are stored in AWS's Mumbai (ap-south-1) region, in India.
Sign-in
Google, for authentication only.

Beyond these, we disclose personal data only when the law requires it.

4. Cookies

We set one cookie, to keep you signed in. It is functional and cannot be turned off without signing out. There are no advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account details and conversations — while your account exists, and for 30 days after you delete it, after which they are erased.
  • Payment and ledger records — 8 years, because Indian tax and accounting law requires it. These are financial records; they survive account deletion.
  • Operational logs — 90 days.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or send you a copy. Email nakama.ai.support@gmail.com from your account address; we act within 30 days. Deleting your account deletes your conversations and uploads. It does not delete payment records we are legally required to keep, and it does not refund unspent credits on its own — ask for the refund separately, under the Refunds Policy.

7. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Credentials and secrets are held in a managed secrets store, not in our source code. Access to production data is limited to the people who operate the service. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the relevant authority, promptly and without being asked.

8. Children

The service is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account here, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and we email account holders. Continuing to use the service after that means you accept the new version.

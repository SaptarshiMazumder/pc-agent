Refunds & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

The short version. If Comfy Penguin does not work for you, email nakama.ai.support@gmail.com and we will refund the money behind whatever credits you have not spent. Credits already spent on model calls are not refundable, because that compute was bought and used.

1. What you are buying

Comfy Penguin sells prepaid credits in packs of US$5, US$10 and US$20. There is no subscription and no recurring charge, so there is nothing to cancel: if you stop buying credits, nothing further is billed. Credits are spent as the agent makes AI model calls on your behalf.

2. When we refund

We refund the unspent portion of a purchase when:

  • the service is broken, unavailable, or does not do what this site says it does;
  • credits were deducted for work that failed through a fault on our side;
  • you were charged twice for the same purchase, or charged without authorising it;
  • you bought credits and changed your mind before spending them — within 7 days of purchase.

Outside those cases we will still look at what happened. The policy is not written to find reasons to say no.

3. What we refund

The money behind your unspent credits. If you bought US$20 and have spent half, the refund is US$10 and the remaining credits are removed from your balance at the same time. A full refund is only possible when nothing has been spent.

The refund is the full rupee/dollar value of those credits. We do not deduct the payment gateway's fee from what goes back to you, even though the gateway keeps it on a refund.

4. What we do not refund

  • Credits already spent. They paid for AI model calls that were made and billed to us.
  • GPU time already used. The minutes a rented machine ran for you are charged to credits as they happen and were paid to the host; they are not refundable, including the ten or so idle minutes after your last activity before the machine is destroyed.
  • Results you did not like. An image or a workflow that ran correctly but is not what you hoped for is not a service failure. If the agent could not produce a working workflow at all, that is — tell us.
  • Credits that have expired. Credits are valid for 12 months from purchase (see the Terms). Expired credits have no refundable value.

5. How to ask

Email nakama.ai.support@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and tell us what went wrong. No form, no reason required for an unspent-credit refund within 7 days.

6. How long it takes

  • We reply within 2 business days.
  • An approved refund is issued within 3 business days of approval.
  • The money reaches your card, bank or UPI account in 5–7 business days after that, depending on your bank. That last leg is the bank's timeline, not ours.

Refunds go back to the original payment method only, through the same payment gateway that took the money. We cannot send a refund to a different card or account, and we cannot pay it out as cash or as a transfer.

7. What happens to your balance

When a refund is issued, the corresponding credits are removed from your account automatically. If you have spent some of them between asking and the refund being processed, we remove what is left and refund only that — your balance is never driven negative and you are never billed for a shortfall.

8. Chargebacks

If something has gone wrong, email us first. We would rather refund you than contest a chargeback, and a chargeback raised without contacting us costs us a fee on top of the refund. We may suspend an account with an open chargeback until it is resolved.

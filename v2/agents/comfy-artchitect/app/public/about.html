About Comfy Penguin

Last updated 22 September 2026

Comfy Penguin designs, runs and repairs ComfyUI workflows — on your own ComfyUI instance, not ours.

You describe what you want to generate. The agent connects to the ComfyUI server you point it at, reads what is actually installed there — the checkpoints, the custom nodes, the samplers — builds a graph against that, submits it, reads whatever the server rejects, fixes it, and runs it again until the result is right. You get back both the images and the workflow file, importable into ComfyUI like any other.

Why it works against your instance

Every ComfyUI install is different. A tool that writes workflows from memory names checkpoints that exist on its author's machine and wires last year's model families the way they used to work. Comfy Penguin reads the machine in front of it before it writes anything, which is the difference between a workflow file and a workflow that runs.

Where that instance lives is your business: a workstation on your desk, a rented GPU on Vast or RunPod, a Modal endpoint. The agent is given a URL and a credential and works with whatever is there. We do not host GPUs and we do not resell compute.

What you pay for

You buy credits, in packs of US$5, US$10 and US$20. Credits are spent on the AI model calls the agent makes while it is reasoning, researching a model family and repairing your graph — not on the image generation itself, which runs on your own hardware at your own cost. Credits are drawn down as you use them, at the rate shown in the app; nothing is charged on a schedule and there is no subscription to cancel.

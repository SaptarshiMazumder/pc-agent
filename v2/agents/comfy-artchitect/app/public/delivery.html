Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

Nothing is shipped. Comfy Penguin is a software service. There are no physical goods, no packaging and no courier. What you buy is credit on your account, and it is delivered electronically, immediately.

1. What is delivered

Credits. When a payment succeeds, the credits from that pack are added to the account you signed in with and are immediately usable at comfypenguin.com. Nothing is downloaded or installed, and no licence key is sent.

2. When

Immediately — within a minute of the payment succeeding. The payment gateway notifies us the moment the charge clears and the balance updates on its own. You do not need to refresh, re-enter anything or contact us to claim it.

3. If the balance does not appear

Very occasionally a gateway notification is delayed. If your balance has not updated within 30 minutes of a successful payment, email nakama.ai.support@gmail.com with the email address you signed in with and the time of the payment. We will either deliver the credits or refund the payment in full — see the Refunds & Cancellation Policy. You will not be charged for a purchase that was never delivered.

4. Where we deliver

Everywhere. The service is available over the internet wherever it is lawful to use it; there are no delivery regions, no shipping charges and no customs or duties, because nothing crosses a border.

5. How long credits last

Credits are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. See the Terms & Conditions.

# Everyday carry

The product as part of her day — taken out of a bag, picked up off a desk or a vanity, slipped
into a pocket. It sells the product as something you reach for every day.

Best for: perfume, lip products, hand cream, earbuds and headphones, phones and cases, small
gadgets.

Shots:
1. Hook — her hand reaches into frame and picks the product up from where it lives: a vanity
   tray, a desk beside a laptop, the top of an open tote.
2. Detail — the product in her hand at its real size, label to the camera, the place around it
   real and a little lived-in.
3. Payoff — she uses it or puts it on and moves on with her day: a dab of cream, earbuds in, a
   glance back at the camera.

Look: true daylight in a real place — a bright desk by a window, a bathroom counter, a café
table. Honest colour, real clutter kept to a few things. Camera: close to medium, eye level,
gentle and slow.

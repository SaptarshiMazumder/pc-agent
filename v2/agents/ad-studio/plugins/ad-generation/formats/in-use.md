# In use

The product doing what it does, on her — the moment of using it is the ad. It answers "what is
it like" before anyone asks.

Best for: hand and body lotion, skincare, perfume, cosmetics, headphones and earbuds.

Shots:
1. Hook — the first touch: lotion pressed onto the back of her hand, a spray of perfume at the
   wrist, headphones settling over her ears, a lipstick meeting the lip.
2. Detail — the product in her other hand or just beside her, label to the camera, at its real
   size; the texture of the cream, the mist in the light, the earcup under her fingers.
3. Payoff — her reaction: eyes closing for a breath, a small smile, a nod to the music; the
   product still in frame.

Look: natural, motivated light — a bathroom window, morning light in a bedroom, a city street
in open shade. Real skin texture, no glow. Camera: close, handheld steady, one slow push-in at
most. Motion stays small and slow: one press, one spray, one gesture.

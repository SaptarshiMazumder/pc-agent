# Gift box, top down

The product laid in an open gift box, seen from directly above — the way a jewellery or
accessories store shows a piece the moment the lid comes off. No person.

Best for: jewellery sets, watches, small leather goods, perfume, anything given as a gift.

Shot: straight top-down, the open box centred and filling most of the frame, the product
arranged inside on the box's lining (satin, velvet, suede) the way it would be packed — a
necklace curved around the lining, earrings set either side. The box sits on a real surface
(glass, marble, wood, linen) with a little of it showing around the edges.

Look: bright but natural daylight, as by a store window — soft, with real, slightly uneven
shadows under the lid and the box walls, gentle true reflections in metal and glass, no glow.
Honest colour; the lining's real texture and small creases; nothing too perfect.

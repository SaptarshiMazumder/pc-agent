# Flat lay

The product laid flat and shot from straight above, with a few complementary items around it —
the editorial overhead layout. No person.

Best for: clothing, scarves, jewellery sets, bags with their contents, accessory edits.

Shot: straight top-down; the product the largest and most central item, laid naturally (a
garment folded or loosely spread, a necklace curved), a few small props at the edges; a real
surface underneath (linen, paper, wood, stone).

Look: soft natural window light raking gently across from one side, so fabric texture, folds and
metal relief show with real, soft shadows. Honest colour; small natural irregularities — a
crease, a thread — instead of a too-perfect arrangement.

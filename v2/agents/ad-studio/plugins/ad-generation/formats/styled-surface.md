# Styled surface

The product on a real surface in a real place — a glass table, a marble counter, a wooden
dresser, linen on a bed — with one or two quiet props that tell where it lives. No person.

Best for: jewellery, accessories, bags, shoes, beauty, home objects — anything that looks better
in context than on a plain background.

Shot: the product the clear subject, close enough to read its details, at eye level or a slight
high angle; the surface and props in the frame but softly secondary; the background falling out
of focus.

Look: daylight from a nearby window, with the real shadows and reflections the surface gives (a
glass table doubles the product faintly; marble shows a soft sheen). Lived-in, not staged —
nothing perfectly symmetrical, nothing glowing.

# Lookbook turn

A clean, editorial lookbook: the model poses and turns slowly to show the product from every
angle, like a brand campaign.

Best for: clothing, full looks, bags, shoes.

Shots:
1. Hook — a striking pose facing the camera, then a slow half-turn; wind or movement in the
   fabric.
2. Angle — a full-body view from the side or back, showing how the product sits and moves.
3. Payoff — a medium shot, she turns back to camera and holds a confident pose with the product
   front and centre.

Look: a seamless studio backdrop in a colour that complements the product, or a minimalist
architectural location (concrete, arches, a white stair). High-end campaign lighting, crisp,
controlled. Camera: static or a very slow orbit.

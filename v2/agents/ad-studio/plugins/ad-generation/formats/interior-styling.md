# Interior styling

The product placed in a room people want to live in — the way home brands shoot their
catalogues. No model needed; at most a hand.

Best for: vases, lamps, candles, cushions, throws, rugs, mirrors, prints, ceramics, tableware.

Shots:
1. Hook — a slow push-in across a beautiful interior toward the product, which is the room's
   focal point.
2. Detail — close-up on the product's texture or finish, light moving across it (a candle
   flame, sun through a window, a lamp switching on).
3. Payoff — a hand places, touches or lights the product; it sits centred and sharp.

Look: natural window light, a considered palette (warm neutrals, earthy tones, or a bold
accent that matches the product), styled but lived-in, architectural-digest photography.
Camera: slow, smooth, gliding moves.

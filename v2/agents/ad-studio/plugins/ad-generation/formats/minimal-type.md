# Minimal type

A few words in big, beautiful type carry the ad; the product sits small and elegant, like in a
magazine. Quiet confidence.

Best for: premium and luxury products, fragrance, jewellery, skincare, design objects.

Layout: the headline set very large across most of the frame (it may wrap onto two or three
lines); the product small, placed in the open space at the bottom or to one side; the call to
action tiny and understated at the bottom.

Look: a single flat background colour or a soft paper texture; one typeface; at most two colours
besides the product's own; a real, soft shadow under the product.

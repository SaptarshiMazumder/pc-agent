# Flat-lay to worn

Starts on the product styled on a surface, then cuts to it being worn — "how to style it" in
three shots. Satisfying and shoppable.

Best for: accessories, jewellery, small bags, scarves, sunglasses, belts.

Shots:
1. Hook — top-down flat-lay: the product arranged with a few complementary items on a textured
   surface; a hand reaches in and picks the product up.
2. Transition — close-up as she puts it on or clips it closed.
3. Payoff — the full look: she wears it in a matching setting, looking at camera.

Look: soft daylight, a tidy aesthetic surface (linen, travertine, wood), a colour story that
matches the product. Camera: overhead for the flat-lay, then eye level.

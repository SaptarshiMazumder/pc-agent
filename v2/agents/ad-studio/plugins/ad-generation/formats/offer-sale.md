# Sale poster

The offer is the ad: the number or the deal set huge, the product beside it, the call to action
underneath. Only when the user gave an offer.

Best for: discounts, bundles, free shipping, seasonal sales — any product.

Layout: the offer in the largest type on the poster (e.g. a big "30% OFF" block or badge); the
headline smaller above or below it; the product to one side, large enough to recognise at a
glance; the call to action on a button at the bottom; fine print, if given, small along the
bottom edge.

Look: high contrast and energy — a bold background colour, a badge or a stripe behind the offer,
one strong accent colour. Still clean: never more than four pieces of text.

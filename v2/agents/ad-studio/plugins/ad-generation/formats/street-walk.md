# Street walk

The model walks toward camera down a beautiful street wearing or carrying the product — the
"main character" energy that fashion Reels run on.

Best for: bags, shoes, outerwear, sunglasses, jewellery that catches light, full outfits.

Shots:
1. Hook — a low or mid-height tracking shot, she walks straight at the camera mid-stride, the
   product swinging or catching light. Confident, not posed.
2. Detail — close-up on the product in motion (the bag against her hip, the shoe hitting the
   cobbles, sunlight flashing on the earring).
3. Payoff — she stops, turns her head to camera or glances back over her shoulder; the product
   is fully visible and centred.

Look: golden hour or bright overcast, a street with character (European old town, Tokyo side
street, palm-lined boulevard), shallow depth of field, editorial street-style photography.
Camera: tracking backwards with her, or a slow push-in; never a static wide.

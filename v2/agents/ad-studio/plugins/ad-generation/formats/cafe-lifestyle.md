# Café lifestyle

The product in a moment people want to live: a beautiful café, a terrace, a morning in the
city. It sells the life around the product.

Best for: bags, sunglasses, jewellery, casual clothing, phone accessories.

Shots:
1. Hook — she sits down at a sunny café table, sets the product down beside her coffee (or
   slides the sunglasses up into her hair); a little laugh or glance.
2. Detail — close-up of the product on the table or on her, with the coffee cup, the light
   through leaves, the texture of the table.
3. Payoff — she picks it up, stands and walks off toward the street; the product swings into
   full view.

Look: warm morning or late-afternoon light, a terrace in Paris or a Mediterranean town,
candid documentary style, shallow depth of field. Camera: handheld, eye level, gentle motion.

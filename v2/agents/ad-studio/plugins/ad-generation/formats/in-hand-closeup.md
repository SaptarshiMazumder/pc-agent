# In-hand close-up

The product held up to the camera, close to her face — the way someone shows a friend what they
just bought. Her face sells the feeling; the product sells itself.

Best for: perfume, skincare bottles and jars, lotion, lipstick and compacts, earbuds cases,
phones, anything small enough to hold up in one hand.

Shots:
1. Hook — she lifts the product into frame beside her cheek, label to the camera, and meets the
   lens with a small smile.
2. Detail — the product fills more of the frame, still in her hand; her fingers around its
   sides or base, the label sharp and readable, her face soft behind it.
3. Payoff — a slow turn of the wrist so the light slides across it, and it settles back, label
   to the camera.

Look: soft window light or open shade from one side, real skin, a simple background that lets
the product read (a bathroom shelf, a bedroom wall, a café window). Camera: eye level, 50–85mm,
close, almost still.

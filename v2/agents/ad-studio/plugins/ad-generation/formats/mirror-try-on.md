# Mirror try-on

The "fit check": the model tries the product on in front of a mirror or at a window and admires
it — relatable, native to the feed, and it shows how the product looks worn.

Best for: clothing, jackets, dresses, hats, jewellery, sunglasses, watches.

Shots:
1. Hook — she puts the product on in one motion (slips the jacket over her shoulders, clicks the
   watch shut, slides the glasses on), looking at her reflection.
2. Turn — she turns side to side, checking the fit; the product catches the light.
3. Payoff — she steps closer to the mirror or the camera, a small satisfied smile; the product
   fills the frame.

Look: a stylish bedroom or boutique fitting room, soft window light, warm neutral palette,
clean and aspirational but lived-in. Camera: static or gentle handheld, eye level.

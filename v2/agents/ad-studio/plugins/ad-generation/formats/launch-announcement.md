# Launch announcement

A new product's first appearance: "New" or "Introducing" with its name, the product presented
like a premiere.

Best for: new products, new colours, new collections.

Layout: a small kicker word ("New", "Introducing", "Just dropped") above the headline; the
headline (often the product's name) large; the product below or beside it, lit like a reveal on
a clean surface; the call to action small at the bottom.

Look: premium and calm — a deep or soft single-colour background, elegant type, a gentle glow of
real light on the product, lots of space. The kicker counts as text: it is part of the headline
or the subline, never extra words.

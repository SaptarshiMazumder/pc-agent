# Hero poster

The product as the hero, big and centred, with a bold headline that says what it gives you and a
clear call to action. The classic product ad.

Best for: any product with a strong look — bottles, skincare, gadgets, jewellery, bags.

Layout: the headline across the top third in large bold type; the product big in the middle,
standing on a simple surface with a soft real shadow; the subline (if any) under the headline;
the call to action on a pill-shaped button at the bottom.

Look: a plain or softly graded background in one of the product's own colours or a colour that
sets it off; two type styles at most; lots of space; the product sharp and true to its photos.

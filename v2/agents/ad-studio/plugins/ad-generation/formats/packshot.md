# Packshot

The product alone on a plain, seamless surface — the clean catalogue image a shop listing leads
with. No person, no props.

Best for: any product a shopper needs to see exactly: shape, colour, finish, every detail.

Shot: the whole product, centred, filling about two-thirds of the frame, at the angle that shows
it best (straight on, three-quarter, or top down for flat items); a light, neutral or softly
coloured background that suits it.

Look: soft natural light from a large window or overcast sky; a real contact shadow under the
product and a soft falloff across the background — never a flat, shadowless cut-out. True
colour and real material texture.
